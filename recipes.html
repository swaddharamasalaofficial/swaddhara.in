Bengali Kosha Mangsho

ঐতিহ্যবাহী খাসির কষা মাংস

Prep

30 Min

Cook

60 Min

Serves

4-5

Slow-Cooked Rich Mutton Gravy

The hallmark of true Bengali Kosha Mangsho is its dark, glistening, aromatic gravy that clings lovingly to every succulent tender meat piece.

Swaddhara Spices Secret:

  • • Swaddhara Kashmiri Mirch (2 tbsp): Gives that signature dark red-brown glaze without overpowering heat.
  • • Swaddhara Jeera Powder (1 tbsp): Adds deep, roasted, rustic aroma to the slow caramelization.
  • • Swaddhara Turmeric & Red Chilli: For balanced pungency and marination base.

Chef's Method:

Marinate mutton with mustard oil, yogurt, Swaddhara Turmeric, and Red Chilli for 2 hours. In a heavy iron kadai, fry sliced onions till golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and Swaddhara Kashmiri Mirch & Jeera powder dissolved in warm water. Slow roast (koshano) till oil separates cleanly.

Hilsa in Golden Mustard Gravy

Nothing matches the fragrant royal taste of pristine Hilsa fish cooked gently with cold-pressed mustard paste and pure golden turmeric.

Swaddhara Spices Secret:

  • • Swaddhara Turmeric Powder (1.5 tsp): High curcumin creates the brilliant radiant sunshine hue without bitterness.
  • • Swaddhara Red Chilli Powder (1/2 tsp): Balances the mustard pungency with clean warmth.

Chef's Method:

Rub fresh Ilish steaks with Swaddhara Turmeric and sea salt. Mix ground black & yellow mustard with water and a pinch of salt. Temper pure mustard oil with kalonji and slit green chillies. Pour the mustard mixture and slide in fish. Simmer gently for 8 minutes to keep the fish buttery soft.

Sweet & Spicy Baby Potato Curry

No onion, no garlic, yet overflowing with deep spice aroma and irresistible richness—the crowning dish of Bengali winter pujas and celebrations.

Swaddhara Spices Secret:

  • • Swaddhara Coriander Powder (2 tsp): Brings cooling herbal citrus fragrance to thicken the tomato-ginger base.
  • • Swaddhara Kashmiri Mirch (1.5 tsp): Provides the festive vermilion color.
  • • Swaddhara Jeera Powder (1 tsp): Roasted earthy notes finish the curry.

Chef's Method:

Boil and prick baby potatoes, then golden-fry with a pinch of turmeric. In ghee, temper cumin seeds, bay leaf, and whole garam masala. Sauté tomato-ginger paste with Swaddhara Coriander, Kashmiri Mirch, and Jeera powder until oil leaves the sides. Add potatoes and simmer with warm water on dum for 15 minutes.
