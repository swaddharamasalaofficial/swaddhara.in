Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Swaddhara spices 100% free from artificial colors?

Yes, absolutely. We do not use Sudan dyes, Metanil yellow, or artificial chemical coloring agents in any of our spices. The vivid redness of our Kashmiri Mirch and the golden radiance of our Turmeric are 100% natural, tested and certified.

What is the shelf life of sealed Swaddhara packets?

Due to our airtight, light-shielding aroma-lock multi-layer foil packaging, all Swaddhara ground spices maintain peak freshness, flavor potency, and essential oil levels for 12 months from the date of manufacture.

How can grocery stores or supermarkets stock Swaddhara?

Retailers can visit our Dealership Portal or email us at swaddharamasala@gmail.com with your shop address and requirements. Our regional sales representative will visit with sample kits and wholesale price catalogues.
